Buyer's Technical Advice

Which CCTV System Suits Your Property?

An honest breakdown of technologies, trade-offs, and practical considerations for Carindale property owners.

GOLD STANDARD

Wired PoE

Continuous local recording and a stable data connection for permanent installations. Powered over a single Cat6 ethernet cable directly from the central NVR.

✓ 24/7 Continuous: Never misses a second of incident footage.
✓ Zero Wi-Fi Jams: Immune to wireless signal jamming and interference.
✓ $0 Subscriptions: All video stored locally on your hard drive with zero cloud bills.
Best For: Detached houses, townhouses, retail shops & commercial offices.
FLEXIBLE OPTION

Wi-Fi or Battery

Useful where cabling is impractical, with power, signal and subscription trade-offs explained upfront before purchase.

• Rental Friendly: Easy to remove and take with you when moving properties.
⚠ Battery Charging: Requires periodic ladder access to recharge every 2-3 months.
⚠ Motion Lag: Camera only starts recording 1-2 seconds after wake-up sensor trips.
Best For: Short-term rental homes, solid double-brick units & temporary job sites.
OPTICS & INTELLIGENCE

Camera Features

Resolution, lens angle, IR or full-colour night vision, detection, deterrence and audio settings customized for your layout.

👁️ 4K vs 4MP: 4K provides digital zoom to read number plates and facial features.
🌈 ColorVu Night: F1.0 super-aperture captures vivid clothing color in pitch black.
🧠 AcuSense AI: Smart target filter categorizes humans and vehicles, ignoring trees.
Best For: Property owners seeking police-grade evidentiary detail.
